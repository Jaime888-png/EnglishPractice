Practise B2 English Grammar

These B2 English grammar exercises are designed to help you practise important grammar structures commonly used at upper-intermediate level.

Each question gives you immediate feedback and an explanation, so you can understand your mistakes and improve your accuracy.

What grammar topics can you practise?

Verb Tenses

Practise past, present and future forms, including perfect and continuous tenses.

Conditionals

Review first, second and third conditionals and improve your ability to talk about possibilities and hypothetical situations.

Modal Verbs

Practise modal verbs for advice, obligation, possibility and deduction.

Reported Speech

Work on reported statements and questions using accurate English grammar.

Gerunds & Infinitives

Practise common verb patterns such as suggest doing, regret doing and regret to do.

Relative Clauses

Improve your use of relative pronouns such as who, which and whose.

Why practise B2 grammar?

Strong grammar is an important part of communicating clearly in English. Regular practice can help you recognise common structures, avoid mistakes and use more accurate English.

B2 grammar practice can also be useful for students preparing for English exams such as the Cambridge B2 First.

How it works

Choose a quiz length, answer the questions and receive instant feedback. At the end of the practice, you can see your result and continue practising.
